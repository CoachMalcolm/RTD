# How to use the RTD

Tips, tricks and hints for getting the most out of your Running To Do.

## The method in 30 seconds

1. **Capture everything.** If it's on your mind, it goes in a block. Use the capture bar at the top of List, or the "+ Add to…" line at the bottom of any block.
2. **Write the next action.** Not "Dentist", but "Call Dr. Patel's office". The item is the *what*. The next action is the very next physical step.
3. **Cross it off. Never delete it.** Crossed-off items stay visible with a strike-through. That's your journal and your proof of progress.
4. **Let it roll.** Anything not crossed off carries into next week with a **wk 2**, **wk 3**… badge. If something keeps rolling, it's telling you something.
5. **WMtMS: whatever makes the most sense.** Rename blocks, add statuses and reshape your day whenever your life changes. The system serves you, not the other way round.

## A good rhythm

**Each morning (2 minutes)**
- Look at **Coming up** on the List tab: today's events and any heads-up reminders.
- Check the **"need you"** button in the header. It counts waiting items that have gone quiet plus heads-ups that are due.
- Pick the two or three next actions that matter today. Plan them on the Days tab if it helps.

**During the day**
- Capture fast and sort later. Type, pick a block, press Enter.
- Tap an item to log what happened: **lvm**, **Called**, **Texted**, **Emailed**, **Met**, **Paid**, or type your own note.
- Cross things off as you go. The circle fills in with the block's color.

**Once a week (10 to 15 minutes, same day each week)**
- Open **Patterns**. What's rolling 3+ weeks? Either do it, break it into a smaller next action, or decide it doesn't belong.
- Check **Waiting on others**. Follow up or give it more time on purpose.
- Read the **Journal** for the week. It's your record of what actually happened.
- Tap **›** in the header to look at next week's events.
- Optional: Journal → **Save a printable summary (PDF)**, or **Copy my week as text** and paste it into an AI chat for a second opinion.

## Blocks

- Blocks are boxes for whatever is on your plate *right now*: projects, clients, life areas, a season of training.
- **Setup → Blocks:** rename, tap the color dot to change color, reorder with ↑ ↓, or add a block.
- A block can only be removed when it's empty. Move its items first: tap an item, change **Block**.
- **Tip:** fewer blocks is usually better. If a block has nothing in it for a few weeks, retire it.
- **Tip:** a "Personal" or "Life admin" block catches the dentist, the bills and the car.

## Items

Tap any item to open it.

| Field | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Item** | The thing, in a few words |
| **WWW** | Who, where, what, why: names, numbers, options you're weighing |
| **Next action** | The very next step |
| **Status** | Open, a waiting state, or Done |
| **Block** | Move it to another block |
| **Plan it for a day** | Put it on the Days tab in a time-of-day block |
| **Repeats every week** | For things you do weekly; crossing off counts only for that week |
| **Log** | Your running notes for this item, with date and time |

**Hints**
- **Phone numbers, emails and web links** in WWW or next action become tappable, with a **Copy** button.
- Tapping **lvm** on an open item logs "lvm" *and* sets it to **wcb** (waiting for callback) in one go.
- Changing a status is logged automatically, so the journal shows "Status → wcb" with the time.
- **Delete…** exists for true mistakes. For anything you actually did, cross it off instead so it stays in your history.

## Waiting states

- **wth:** waiting to hear back. **wcb:** waiting for callback.
- Each has a **Nudge after** number of days (Setup → Statuses). After that, the badge turns red with the days waiting, for example **wcb · 6d**.
- Add your own in Setup → Statuses, for example **wp**, "Waiting on payment", nudge after 7 days.

## Days and time of day

- Each day is split into time-of-day blocks. The defaults are Early AM 5–9a, Morning 9a–12n, Afternoon 12–3p, Late afternoon 3–6p, Evening 6–9p and Night 9p–1a.
- **Setup → Time of day:** rename them, change the times, reorder, add or remove.
- Tap any block on the Days tab to jot in it. Tap away to save.
- On a phone you see one day at a time. Use the day strip at the top to switch.
- **Weekly to-do** at the top of Days is for notes about the whole week.

## Repeating events

Tap **+ Event**, on the List tab (Coming up) or the Days tab.

- **Time** is optional. Without one, pick a time of day such as "Evening".
- **Repeats:** daily, every weekday, weekly on chosen days (every 1 to 6 weeks), monthly (on the 21st, or on the "3rd Wednesday"), or yearly.
- **Ends:** never, on a date, or after a number of times.
- **Heads-up:** a reminder 1 to 14 days before, with a to-do such as "Confirm the appointment". It shows in Coming up until you tap Done.

Tap any event to open just that date:
- **Mark done ✓** or **Skip this one**. Both go into the Journal.
- **Note for this one only:** "moved to 7am", "bring the bands".
- **End the series after this:** stops a repeating event cleanly and keeps its history.
- Below that, **The whole series** edits every date.

**Hints**
- Use a block on events (Training, Personal…) so they carry the block's color.
- Put an address or link in **Where** so it's one tap from the event.
- Add a yearly event "Renew RTD sync key" with a 7-day heads-up.

## Weeks

- **‹ ›** in the header move between weeks. **Today** jumps back.
- Looking at a past week shows what was open then and what you crossed off that week.
- Adding an item while viewing another week dates it into that week.
- Setup → Week lets you start weeks on Monday or Sunday.

## Journal and Patterns

- **Journal:** every cross-off, log note, status change and event check-off for the week, grouped by day with times.
- **Patterns:**
  - **When you finish things:** completions by weekday, all time. Use it to plan hard tasks for your strong days.
  - **What keeps rolling:** the open items carried the longest.
  - **Waiting on others:** everyone who owes you a reply, longest first.
  - **This week by block:** how much of each block you got through.

## Getting help from AI without paying for it

Journal → **Copy my week as text** puts the whole week on your clipboard (blocks, items, statuses, how long things have rolled, recent log notes and events). Paste it into any AI chat, including free ones, with a prompt like:

- "Review my week. What's stalling and what should I do first tomorrow?"
- "Draft a short follow-up text for each item I'm waiting on."
- "Which of these could I drop or hand off?"

## Small tricks

- **Enter** adds an item from any capture line. The cursor stays put, so you can add several in a row.
- **Escape** closes whatever is open (an item, an event, Setup).
- The header's **W43** box is the week number, just like the corner of the old spreadsheet.
- The sync status under the dates tells you what's happening: "Synced 9:14a", "Syncing…", "Offline · will sync later", or "Sync key expired".
- **Export backup** once a month and keep it in Drive or on your laptop. It's one small file with everything.

## Your first day

1. Tap **Clear examples**.
2. Setup → Blocks: make them match what's on your plate this month.
3. Dump everything on your mind into the capture bar. Don't sort, just capture.
4. Add your recurring sessions and appointments as events.
5. Turn on sync and add the app to your home screen.
6. Tomorrow morning, start the rhythm above.
