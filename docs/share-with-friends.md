## First-time setup

### 1. Open the app

Open **YOUR-LINK** on your phone:
- **iPhone:** use **Safari**. It works best for adding the app to your home screen.
- **Android:** use **Chrome**.

You'll see a sample week of example items so you can see how it works.

### 2. Put the RTD icon on your home screen

This step matters. It makes the RTD open full screen like a regular app, lets it work with no signal, and on iPhone it keeps the phone from clearing your list.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen. It's a square with an arrow pointing up. On some iPhones it's at the top right, or behind the **⋯** button.
2. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, tap **Edit Actions…** and add it.
3. The name will say **RTD**. Tap **Add** at the top right.
4. Find the blue-gray **RTD** icon on your home screen and open the app from there from now on.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
2. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
3. Tap **Add** or **Install**.
4. Open the RTD from the new icon.

**Mac or PC (optional)**
- **Safari on Mac:** File menu → **Add to Dock**.
- **Chrome or Edge:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ → **Install RTD**.
- Or just bookmark the link.

### 3. Make it yours

1. Tap **Clear examples**.
2. Tap ⚙ **Setup** → **Blocks**. Rename them to what's on your plate: work, a side project, family, training, whatever makes the most sense. Tap a color dot to change its color.
3. Back on **List**, type everything on your mind into the capture bar, pick a block, and press **Add**.
4. Add anything that repeats (practices, classes, appointments) with **+ Event**.

### 4. Optional: sync your phone and computer

Without sync, each device keeps its own separate list. To share one list across your devices:

1. Create a free account at **github.com** if you don't have one.
2. In the RTD, tap ⚙ **Setup** → **Sync your iPhone and MacBook** and tap the link to GitHub's key page.
3. Leave only **gist** ticked, set **Expiration** (90 days to 1 year), and tap **Generate token**.
4. Copy the key (it starts with `ghp_`), paste it into the RTD, and tap **Connect**.
5. On your other device, open the same link and paste the same key.

Your list is stored in a secret file in **your** GitHub account, not mine. When the key expires, the app tells you, and you paste in a new one. Nothing is lost.
